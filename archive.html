Rich Palmer The Archive

A back catalogue of older projects. Design trends move on and some of this looks its age, but the thinking behind each piece still stands. I've kept them in the portfolio because they show the breadth of clients and sectors I've worked across over the years. Sport, fashion, retail, hospitality, security, construction and plenty in between.

All the hits (and maybe some misses too). Some of these projects pushed me into unfamiliar territory, others taught me lessons I still rely on today. Viewed together they give the full picture of a career built on solving very different problems for very different people, not just the highlights that make the front page.
